## Theme policy

Original is the only public/production theme. Keep the Water palette, styles,
components and animations available for development previews and future use.
Never restore public theme controls or read a saved theme in production.
Make shared content and functionality changes for both themes where practical,
but target visual refinements at Original; do not add Water-specific polish
unless needed to keep shared components working. Preserve approved copy and
existing enquiry submission/email logic unless explicitly requested otherwise.
